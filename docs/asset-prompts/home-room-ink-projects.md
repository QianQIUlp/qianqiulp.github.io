目标：点击「作品」热点后的近景——右侧工作台/工具墙特写，扳手、图纸、放大灯，
是"动手构建"的区域。

## Canvas

- 与现网 projects 近景一致（横构图，约 3:2）。

## Prompt

> Close-up of the maker workbench in the same jiehua ink-wash study: a wooden
> workbench under a swing-arm magnifier lamp, pegboard behind with hanging tools,
> rolled blueprints and a small cinnabar-stamped tag (#a2402c, only color accent),
> electronic components and a soldering iron resting. Same single light from upper
> left, shadows to lower right, five ink tonal grades on xuan paper texture, edges
> dissolving into soft ink vignette. Quiet craftsmanship mood, no readable text.

## Negative

> readable text, colorful plastic tools, LED strips, multiple light sources,
> hard rectangular frame, white background, watermark, messy clutter
