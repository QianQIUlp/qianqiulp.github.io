# home-room-ink-curtain · 幕布近景

目标：点击「生活」热点后的近景——左侧深红丝绒幕布与窗边一角的特写，
暗示舞台与生活的另一面。

## Canvas

- 与现网 curtain 近景一致（横构图，约 3:2）。

## Prompt

> Close-up of the deep red velvet curtain at the left edge of the same jiehua
> ink-wash study, heavy folds catching a single beam of warm lamplight from upper
> left, a glimpse of the dark window and plants behind, floor shadow pooling toward
> lower right. The curtain red is deeply desaturated wine-ink, harmonized with five
> ink tonal grades on xuan paper; no other color accents. Edges dissolve into ink
> vignette, theatrical yet quiet, no readable text.

## Negative

> bright scarlet, stage spotlights from multiple directions, people, readable text,
> hard border, white background, watermark

## Checklist

- [ ] 幕布红压暗为"绛墨"，不刺眼
- [ ] 与 overview 最左边缘幕布连续
- [ ] 光源左上、投影右下
- [ ] 边缘墨化
